The [Digest Fields](/spec/security/digest-fields/) page now explains `Unencoded-Digest`, which checks a complete representation after decompression. It documents Chrome and Edge support, the Firefox and Safari gap, and the separate `Want-Unencoded-Digest` preference field; the topic remains `optional`.
