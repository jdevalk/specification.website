---
title: "Color-scheme page now covers browsers darkening undeclared pages"
date: "2026-11-13"
type: changed
relatedSlugs: [color-scheme]
---

The [color-scheme](/spec/foundations/color-scheme/) page now covers browsers darkening pages that declare no scheme: Chrome on Android's Auto Dark Theme and Safari 27.3's Adaptive Dark Mode, which inverts luminosity on pages it judges light. It also documents `only light` as the opt-out from both, notes Safari 27.3 beta 1 does not honour the `only` keyword yet, and dates the Baseline status of `color-scheme` and `light-dark()`.
