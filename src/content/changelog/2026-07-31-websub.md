Added a page on [WebSub](/spec/foundations/websub/), the W3C Recommendation for pushing feed updates to subscribers rather than making them poll. It was republished on 2 June 2026 with new cross-site-scripting mitigations in its security considerations. The page covers the publisher's side — advertising `rel="hub"` and `rel="self"`, and pinging the hub on publish — and is `optional`, since it only applies if you publish a feed and have a hub to point at.
