The spec now has a [customizable select page](/spec/foundations/customizable-select/): opt a real `<select>` into `appearance: base-select` for full picker styling and rich options, as a progressive enhancement — Chrome and Edge since April 2025, Safari since September 2026, Firefox not yet. From the Modern Web Guidance review.
