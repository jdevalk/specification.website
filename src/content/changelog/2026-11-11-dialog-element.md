The spec now has a [dialog element page](/spec/foundations/dialog-element/): native `<dialog>` with `showModal()` for genuinely modal UI, `closedby` as the dismissal policy, and the boundary against popover (non-modal) and the hand-rolled `role="dialog"` div (neither). From the Modern Web Guidance review.
