Added an optional page on the [Universal Commerce Protocol](/spec/agent-readiness/universal-commerce-protocol/) and its `/.well-known/ucp` business profile, provisionally registered with IANA on 26 October 2026 and already served by Shopify storefronts. It explains that the profile is a capability manifest for agent checkout, not a product-discovery signal, and covers the spec's hosting rules on redirects and caching.
