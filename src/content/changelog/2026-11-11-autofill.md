The spec now has an [autofill page](/spec/accessibility/autofill/): the WHATWG `autocomplete` token vocabulary as a requirement in its own right — granular fields, section prefixes for shipping and billing, distinct sign-in and sign-up password tokens, card tokens, and `one-time-code` — instead of leaving the topic scattered across the mobile-inputs and redundant-entry pages. From the Modern Web Guidance review.
