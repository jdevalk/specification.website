title: "OKF bundles move provenance into front matter"
date: "2026-09-10"
type: changed
relatedSlugs: [okf-bundle]
---

The Open Knowledge Format has reached v0.2, which supersedes two shapes from v0.1: the flat `timestamp` field becomes `generated.at` alongside a required `generated.by` actor, and the body-level `# Citations` list becomes a front-matter `sources` list. [The OKF bundle page](/spec/agent-readiness/okf-bundle/) now describes both, and explains why the `human:` actor prefix is a trust signal rather than a credit line. Our own [`/okf/`](/okf/index.md) bundle targets 0.2 accordingly.
