relatedSlugs: [robots-for-ai-crawlers, content-signals]

The [robots.txt for AI crawlers page](/spec/agent-readiness/robots-for-ai-crawlers/#a-shared-training-preference) now covers the `__usage_ai-training` token, an Internet-Draft from 30 October 2026 that Microsoft and Google plan to honour in early 2027. The [Content Signals page](/spec/agent-readiness/content-signals/) now makes clear that those commitments are to this token, not to the `Content-Signal` directive.
