---
title: "Anchor positioning is not Baseline yet; position-visibility values renamed"
date: "2026-11-24"
type: changed
relatedSlugs: [anchor-positioning]
---

The [anchor positioning](/spec/foundations/anchor-positioning/) page corrects its support claim: the feature is not Baseline yet, because Firefox has not shipped the renamed `position-visibility` values. It also documents the 2026 rename of `anchors-visible` to `anchor-visible`, which Chrome 157 and Safari 27 accept, and notes the old spellings remain as legacy aliases.
