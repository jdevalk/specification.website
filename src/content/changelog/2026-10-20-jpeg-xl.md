title: "Image optimisation: JPEG XL now ships in Chrome and Firefox"
date: "2026-10-20"
type: changed
relatedSlugs: [image-optimization]
---

The [image optimisation page](/spec/performance/image-optimization/) now covers JPEG XL, which Chrome 155 and Firefox 158 enabled in October 2026, joining Safari. It is not yet Baseline because Edge lacks it, so the page recommends offering it as an extra `<picture>` source next to AVIF rather than replacing AVIF.
