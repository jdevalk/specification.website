---
title: JPEG XL joins the image format chain
date: "2026-10-17"
type: changed
relatedSlugs: [image-optimization]
---

With Firefox 158 enabling it by default on 13 October 2026 and Chrome and Edge 155 shipping it alongside, JPEG XL now decodes in every major engine. [Image optimisation](/spec/performance/image-optimization/) adds it as a further `<source>` in the `<picture>` chain, explains where it differs from AVIF — lossless JPEG transcoding and progressive decoding — and warns that browsers pick the first supported source, not the smallest.
