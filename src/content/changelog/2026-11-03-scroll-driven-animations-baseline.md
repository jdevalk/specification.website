---
title: "Scroll-driven animations reached Baseline"
date: "2026-11-03"
type: changed
relatedSlugs: [scroll-driven-animations]
---

The [scroll-driven animations page](/spec/performance/scroll-driven-animations/) now notes that the feature became Baseline newly available on 27 October 2026, when Firefox 159 shipped it as the last major browser, and warns that older browsers ignore `animation-timeline` entirely. The reading-progress example also gained its missing `from` keyframe, so the bar now starts at `scaleX(0)` instead of relying on the element's unanimated state.
