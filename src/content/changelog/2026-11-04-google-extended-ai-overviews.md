The [robots.txt for AI crawlers page](/spec/agent-readiness/robots-for-ai-crawlers/) now separates two controls that are often conflated. Google-Extended governs training and grounding in Gemini Apps and Vertex AI and has no effect on Search. Staying out of AI Overviews, AI Mode and generative AI in Discover is a separate per-property setting in Search Console, which leaves classic results and ranking alone and does not cover training. The page now says so explicitly and points to meta robots for per-page preview limits.
